## Big features

1. **Cyclone Akvan slams into Odisha** — Landfall near Gopalpur between
   7:30 and 8:30 in the morning, winds 70–80 km/h, weaker than feared.
   Odisha moved 440,461 people to safety and reported no deaths; 2,316
   babies were born in shelters; 47 trains were cancelled. Sources: IMD,
   The New Indian Express, Down to Earth, ABP. Note: an earlier Friday
   figure of about 372,000 evacuated was replaced during the day by the
   minister's evening figure of 440,461, which the page prints.
2. **111 missiles, 404 drones: Kyiv's bridges hit** — Russia's biggest air
   strike of the month overnight: 27 ballistic and 84 cruise missiles and
   404 drones. Eight people were killed. Three bridges over the Dnipro
   were damaged, power failed in parts of the capital, and two missiles
   fell in Poland. Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera, ISW, CNN.
3. **Climate talks close with fossil fuels cut from text** — By the
   scheduled last day in Antalya, the summit's draft had dropped every
   mention of fossil fuels and lost its $120bn adaptation target. The
   35-by-35 electricity goal survived. No consensus was reached. Sources:
   Reuters, The Indian Express. Note: the draft in question was published
   on Thursday 19 November; the summit's scheduled final day was Friday.
